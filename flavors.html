Taste Guide

OWYN Protein Shake Flavors

From chocolate and vanilla to cookies and cream, find the OWYN flavor profile that fits your routine.

OWYN Chocolate Protein Shake

The owyn chocolate protein shake is one of the most searched flavor options for everyday sipping. If you want a richer cocoa profile, look at the owyn dark chocolate protein shake and the owyn chocolate plant protein shake for plant-based chocolate taste without dairy. Compare shake styles on the homepage product line-up.

OWYN Vanilla Protein Shake

An owyn vanilla protein shake works well on its own or as a base for fruit smoothies, iced coffee blends, and oatmeal mix-ins. Vanilla is a flexible choice when you want milder sweetness.

OWYN Cookies and Cream

Fans of dessert-style shakes often search owyn cookies and cream and owyn cookies and cream protein shake. These options aim for a treat-like flavor while staying in a plant-based protein format.

Protein Shakes Strawberry Banana

Fruit-forward shoppers frequently look for protein shakes strawberry banana. OWYN and similar plant protein lines may offer berry or banana-leaning flavors depending on retailer assortment, making this a popular comparison flavor for ready-to-drink routines.

How to Choose a Flavor

  • Choose chocolate when you want a richer, dessert-like shake protein experience.
  • Choose vanilla when you plan to mix with fruit, coffee, or oats.
  • Choose cookies and cream when you want a more indulgent ready-to-drink option.
  • Compare labels if you are switching between shake flavors and powder flavors.
